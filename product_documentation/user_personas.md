# User personas

## Priya — Marketing Manager

- **Goal:** Allocate campaign budget while keeping acquisition efficient.
- **Needs:** Fast campaign/channel comparison, budget pacing, prioritized actions, and a record of decisions.
- **Pain points:** Reports use inconsistent definitions; performance issues surface late.
- **Success:** Can identify an action and supporting metric during a short review.

## Arjun — Marketing Analyst

- **Goal:** Produce trustworthy performance analysis for stakeholders.
- **Needs:** Defined formulas, date/campaign/channel filters, source data, and exportable reports.
- **Pain points:** Manual joins can duplicate spend or revenue; source records need cleaning.
- **Success:** Reproduces a metric from the data model and shares a filtered report.

## Maya — Business/Product Leader

- **Goal:** Understand whether marketing decisions support business growth and whether the analytics product is useful.
- **Needs:** Executive summary, explicit caveats, adoption metrics, and a roadmap.
- **Pain points:** High-level charts lack decision context or omit measurement limitations.
- **Success:** Connects a business question to a metric, action, and follow-up measurement.
