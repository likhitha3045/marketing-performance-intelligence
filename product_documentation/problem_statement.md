# Problem statement

## Context

Marketing managers often review spend, conversions, and revenue in separate channel tools or static spreadsheets. Without a consistent view, comparing campaign efficiency, finding funnel drop-offs, and deciding where to test budget changes takes manual effort.

## Problem

The user needs a reliable, filterable way to answer:

1. How much did we spend and what attributed revenue did campaigns generate?
2. Which campaigns and channels acquire customers efficiently?
3. Where does measured activity drop between impressions, clicks, leads, and conversions?
4. Which customer groups and regions contribute observed revenue?
5. What evidence supports the next optimization action?

## Proposed solution

Marketing Performance & Growth Intelligence combines a validated data pipeline, a relational data model, an interactive dashboard, and recommendation/action tracking. Its recommendations use visible portfolio metrics and thresholds rather than generated, unsupported claims.

## Users and value

- Marketing managers: identify campaigns to review and track decisions.
- Marketing analysts: filter, compare, export, and explain performance.
- Product/business leaders: review growth signals and product usefulness KPIs.

## Scope and limitations

The portfolio prototype uses synthetic data. ROAS is attributed revenue divided by recorded campaign spend; it does not account for margin, overhead, incrementality, or attribution uncertainty. There is no authentication or durable multi-user storage in the demo deployment.
